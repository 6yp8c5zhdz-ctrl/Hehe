# Cat Coin Adventure

A fun browser-based game where you play as a young cat collecting coins and food while navigating from the river to home!

## Game Overview

You are a young cat who starts at a river and must journey home to your family. Along the way, you'll collect coins and food while encountering other cats who may help or hinder your progress.

## How to Play

### Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Read the welcome message and start the training level

### Controls
- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** - Move your cat in all directions
- **SPACE** - Talk to other cats when nearby

### Game Mechanics

#### Objectives
- Collect coins (🪙) and food (🍖) scattered throughout each level
- Navigate from the river (left side) to your home (right side)
- Interact with other cats blocking your path

#### Other Cats
- When you encounter another cat, get close and press **SPACE** to talk
- Friendly cats will let you pass and give you **50 bonus coins**
- Unfriendly cats will send you back to the river to try again
- Each cat has a different personality!

#### Winning
- Reach your home at the right side of the screen
- Enjoy a mini celebration party with confetti and balloons
- Progress through multiple levels with increasing difficulty

## Game Features

### Training Level
- Learn the basics of movement and collection
- No blocking cats - just practice!
- Collect coins and food to get familiar with gameplay

### Multiple Levels
- **Level 1**: Introduction to cat interactions with one friendly cat
- **Level 2**: Two cats with mixed personalities
- **Level 3**: Three cats creating more challenging paths

### Progression System
- Track your coins and food collection
- Each level gets progressively harder
- Complete all levels to win the game!

## Tips and Strategy

1. **Collect Everything**: Gather as many coins and food as possible before heading home
2. **Talk to Everyone**: Approach cats and press SPACE to interact - friendly cats give bonus coins!
3. **Plan Your Route**: If a cat sends you back, try a different path
4. **Stay Alert**: Watch for the "Press SPACE" prompt when near cats
